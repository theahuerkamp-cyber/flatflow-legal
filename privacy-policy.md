## 2. What This Is About

FlatFlow is an app for shared households. This policy describes which personal data is processed when you use the app, why, and which rights you have. We only process data that is needed for the app's functions. There is no advertising, no tracking and no analytics tools.

## 3. What Data We Process

| Data | Purpose |
| ---- | ------- |
| Email address | Registration and login. With "Sign in with Apple" this may be a relay address created by Apple. It is stored in your profile but not shown in the app. |
| Password | Login by email. It is processed only by Firebase Authentication and stored in encrypted form. We cannot see it. |
| User ID (UID) | Assigning your account and your data |
| Name | Display within your household (e.g. tasks, calendar, ranking). With "Sign in with Apple" you confirm it during setup. |
| Household code | Membership of your household |
| Stress level ("mood") and weekly lock | Voluntary self-assessment, used only to distribute chores |
| Tasks (title, effort, assignment, status) | Core function of the app |
| Calendar entries (date, name, activity) | Shared household overview |
| Karma points | Gamification and ranking |
| Avatar (emoji), "bring along" wish, move-in date | Display in your profile. These details are voluntary. |
| Technical connection data (e.g. IP address) | Technically incurred when using Firebase services (data transmission, security). |

Reminders (weekly mood reminder, daily reminder of open tasks) are scheduled locally on your device. The settings stay on your device and are not sent to us.

## 4. Who Can See Your Data

The data of your profile and household (e.g. name, stress level, karma, avatar, tasks, calendar entries) is visible to the members of your household. Access to a household is granted via the household code. Members of other households and third parties outside the app cannot see this data. Please share the household code only with people who should join your household.

## 5. Legal Basis

We process your data to provide the app's functions (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Details such as stress level, avatar, "bring along" wish and move-in date are voluntary. You can change them at any time.

## 6. Service Providers and Recipients

**Google Firebase.** We use Firebase Authentication (login) and Cloud Firestore (data storage) provided by Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC. The database is set up in the europe-west3 region (Frankfurt, Germany). A data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR is in place with Google. A transfer of data to Google LLC in the USA cannot be ruled out completely. Google states that it safeguards such transfers with appropriate measures. More information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

**Apple.** If you use "Sign in with Apple", Apple sends us your name (only if you share it), your email address or a relay address, and an Apple identifier. Apple's privacy policy applies to Apple's processing: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/. Apple is responsible for the download from the App Store.

We do not share data with any other third parties.

## 7. Automated Distribution of Tasks

The app automatically distributes open tasks among the household members using a simple calculation rule, taking the stated stress level into account. This has no legal or similarly significant effect on you. You can change assignments at any time.

## 8. Retention and Deletion

Your data is stored as long as your account exists. You can delete your account yourself at any time: **Profile → tap your avatar → Settings → "Konto löschen" (Delete account)**. This deletes your profile and your account. It also removes assignments to you from the household's tasks and deletes calendar entries under your name. If another member of your household has the same name, entries under that name remain because they cannot be assigned unambiguously. In that case, please send us a short message and we will remove them.

If you use "Sign in with Apple", you can additionally remove the app in your Apple ID settings under "Sign in with Apple".

## 9. Security

Data transmission between the app and the server is encrypted. The database's security rules ensure that only members of a household can access that household's data.

## 10. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** the data stored about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
- **Rectification** of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
- **Erasure** of your data (Art. 17 GDPR), directly in the app via "Delete account"
- **Restriction** of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
- **Data portability** (Art. 20 GDPR)
- **Object** to processing (Art. 21 GDPR)
- **Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority** (Art. 77 GDPR)

For requests, please write to: contra.app@yahoo.com
